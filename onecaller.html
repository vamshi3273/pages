1. Introduction

Welcome to One Caller, an Android phone and dialer app designed to help you manage contacts, view call history, and place and handle calls. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, used, stored, and shared when you use the app.

Please read this policy carefully. If you have questions about our privacy practices, contact us using the email address listed above.

2. Information and Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, One Caller may access the following information on your device.

Contacts

If you grant contact permission, the app accesses contacts to display names, phone numbers, favorites, and contact details and to help you place calls. Contact information is processed on your device for these features. Do not retain this statement if your implementation sends contact data to your servers or another service.

Call logs

If you grant call-log permission and the app is permitted to use it under Android and Google Play requirements, the app accesses call history to show recent calls, call types, times, and related details. Call-log information is used to provide dialer functionality.

Phone and calling features

The app may request phone-related permissions or the role of default phone app to provide calling, call handling, and related dialer features. Android controls the availability of these capabilities. We use granted access only for the app's stated features.

Call recording

If call recording is enabled in your version of the app and on your device, audio may be recorded and saved locally on your device. Recording availability and recording sources can vary by Android version, device manufacturer, carrier, and permissions. Recordings may contain sensitive information. You are responsible for complying with applicable laws and obtaining any consent required before recording a call. Update this section if recordings are uploaded, backed up, or shared by the app.

Notifications

If notification permission is granted, notifications may be used for call status, ongoing calls, or other app features. You can manage notification permission in Android settings.

Internet access

The app may use internet access for advertising and any network-dependent features that are enabled. Local dialer features should not require uploading contacts or call logs unless a feature clearly explains otherwise.

5. How We Use Information

Information accessed by One Caller may be used to:

  • Display contacts and call history.
  • Enable calling and other dialer functions.
  • Save app preferences and locally stored app data.
  • Provide call recording or caller-identification features when supported and enabled.
  • Display and measure advertisements through third-party advertising services.
  • Respond to support requests that you send to us.
  • Maintain app security, reliability, and performance.

6. Data Storage, Sharing, and Retention

Contacts and call logs accessed for core dialer functions are intended to be processed on your device. App preferences and any call recordings created by the app may also be stored locally. Local information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, remove the relevant item, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and device settings.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send contacts, call logs, or call recordings to our own servers unless a feature explicitly states otherwise and the app is implemented accordingly. Third-party services, including advertising providers, may process data as described in their own policies.

Third-party providers determine their own retention practices for information they receive. We do not control their retention periods.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. Information stored locally is subject to the security of your device and Android's protections. Data transmitted by the app or its third-party SDKs should use encrypted connections where supported. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • Permissions: Review or revoke permissions in your Android settings. Some features may stop working if permission is removed.
  • Local data: Delete individual recordings or other locally saved items using available app controls. You may also clear One Caller's app data or uninstall the app, noting that Android backups may behave separately.
  • Advertising: Manage available advertising and privacy settings through your device and Google account. Where required, respond to the app's consent choices.
  • Support: Contact us at the email address at the top of this policy with privacy questions or requests concerning data that we control.

If One Caller does not maintain accounts or personal data on our servers, there may be no server account to delete. This does not replace the need to provide appropriate controls for data that the app or its services actually collect.

9. Children's Privacy

One Caller is a general-purpose phone utility and is not specifically designed for children. The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through a support channel, please contact us so we can review the request.
